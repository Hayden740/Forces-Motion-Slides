# Forces and Motion Slides

Year 7 Science, Forces and Motion unit: 28 interactive slide lessons over 7 weeks.

Live site: https://hayden740.github.io/Forces-Motion-Slides/

- Use the arrow keys to move between slides, N for teacher notes, T to start or pause a timer, and F for full screen.
- YouTube videos play on the slides when the site is hosted here on GitHub Pages.
- Built-in simulators: tug of war (net force), spring balance (newtons, mass and weight on other planets), toy car ramp (mass and stopping distance practical), parachute drop (air resistance practical), force diagram builder, magnets, and balloon static charge.

Relief lesson: Lesson 13 (Introducing force diagrams) has a standalone student site with a six page self-marking worksheet at https://hayden740.github.io/Forces-Motion-Slides/relief-force-diagrams/

Parachute simulator: a standalone student page (no access to the rest of the slides) at https://hayden740.github.io/Forces-Motion-Slides/parachute-simulator/

Lesson 19 (How forces change motion) has a student-only copy of its slides, with no other lessons and no teacher notes, at https://hayden740.github.io/Forces-Motion-Slides/lesson-19/

Relief version: Lesson 16 (Forces in real life) has a shorter relief version that follows the printed Week 4 worksheet. Open it from the button on the Lesson 16 title slide, or go to https://hayden740.github.io/Forces-Motion-Slides/?relief#l16-1
