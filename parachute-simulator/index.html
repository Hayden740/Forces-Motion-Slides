Parachute Simulator

Does changing one variable on a parachute change how long it takes to fall? Test the standard parachute, then change one variable and compare.

Page 1: Test the standard parachute

  1. Read the standard parachute card below.
  2. Press Drop from 2 m 3 times.
  3. Copy your 3 times and the average onto your worksheet.
  4. When the average appears, go to Page 2.

Page 2: Change one variable

  1. Choose one variable to change. This is your independent variable. Test at least 2 different versions, with 3 drops each.
  2. Keep all the other variables the same as the standard. These are your controlled variables. The green or red box tells you if your test is fair.
  3. Your standard results from Page 1 stay in the table so you can compare.
  4. Copy every result from the table onto your worksheet.

The standard parachute

  • MaterialTablecloth
  • Shape and sizeSquare, 25 cm x 25 cm
  • Strings4 strings, 30 cm long
  • Skydiver mass2 washers
  • Hole in canopyNone
  • Drop height2 metres

Each washer is 5 g. Times change a little each drop, just like a real stopwatch, so always do 3 trials.
