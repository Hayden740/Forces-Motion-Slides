The science in 60 seconds

  • A force is a push or a pull. It is measured in newtons (N).
  • Contact forces need touching: pushes, friction, air resistance, water resistance, the support force from the ground. Non-contact forces work across a gap: gravity (weight), magnetism, static electricity.
  • Balanced forces cancel out, so the motion doesn't change (still stays still, or keeps the same speed). Unbalanced forces change the motion: speeding up, slowing down or changing direction.
  • Net force = the big force take away the smaller opposite force. Example: 80 N up and 30 N down gives 50 N up.
  • A force diagram shows each force as an arrow starting on the object. Longer arrow = bigger force. Every arrow gets a label.
