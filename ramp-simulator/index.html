Toy Car Ramp Simulator

Does the mass of a toy car change how far it rolls after it leaves a ramp? Use this simulator to collect data, then write it up on your worksheet.

Your task

  1. Keep the ramp at 15 cm.
  2. Choose 0 washers. Press Release car 3 times.
  3. Change to 1, 2, 3 and 4 washers. Run 3 trials for each.
  4. Copy every result from the table below onto your worksheet.

The car is 30 g. Each washer adds 5 g. Distance is measured from the bottom of the ramp to the front of the car.
